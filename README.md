## About me

Hey, I'm Huzaifa. I'm a full stack developer who works mostly with the **MERN stack** and **Next.js**, and I spend more time than most developers thinking about how fast a page loads and whether search engines can make sense of it.

My work usually falls into two buckets. The first is building web apps from scratch that hold up once real users arrive. The second is fixing sites that look fine on the surface but keep losing traffic because of slow pages, messy markup or a structure Google can't follow. A good chunk of my freelance work is moving businesses off WordPress and onto Next.js, where performance and SEO can be handled properly instead of patched with plugins.

I've been writing production code since 2022, and since August 2024 I've been building React and MERN apps at Mehdi Technologies.
